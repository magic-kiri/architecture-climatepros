What is Use Case C?

Every repair needs parts, and today the technician has to work out which parts on their own, then hunt for them across their van, nearby warehouses, and outside parts suppliers. Wrong guesses mean second trips; blind sourcing means driving further than necessary, or going to an outside supplier for something already sitting nearby in a colleague's van. Use Case C adds a Parts Finder inside the technician's FieldJetX (FJX 2.2) mobile app that does two things: it predicts the parts a dispatch is likely to need from the dispatch's written reason, and it sources each part across every place it can be gathered - the tech's own van, other ClimatePros warehouses, the branch warehouse, and a nearby outside parts supplier. Prediction is additive and advisory: the technician stays in control and can always add parts manually.

📦 Multi-Source Gather
For every part, surface all stock locations - own inventory first, then nearest warehouses, then an outside parts supplier - so the closest, most-available option is picked first.
🚚 Fewer return trips
Parts are predicted from what similar past jobs actually used, so the tech is less likely to arrive without the part the repair needs.
📍 Closest, most available
Own stock and nearby warehouses are preferred over an outside supplier, ordered by distance - cutting time spent hunting and unnecessary driving.
🔄 Improves automatically
Every completed dispatch is re-embedded into the index, so predictions get better as more work is done - no manual rules to maintain.
